# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Overview
**Product Name:** Reading Sanctuary  
**Description:** A sanctuary for avid readers to organize, read, listen, annotate, and journal their reading journeys.

## Goals & Objectives
- Provide a unified digital library experience (e-books, audiobooks, physical book tracking).
- Enable immersive reading and audio playback with bookmarking and annotations.
- Foster personal reflection via reading journals and progress analytics.

## Core Features
1. **Authentication:** User sign-up, login, password recovery, and profile setup.
2. **Library & Bookshelf:** Custom shelves, status tracking (To Read, Reading, Finished, DNF), filtering, and search.
3. **Reader:** E-book reader supporting customizable themes, fonts, margins, and progress sync.
4. **Audiobook Player:** Audio player with speed controls, chapter navigation, and sleep timer.
5. **Annotations & Highlights:** Highlight text, attach notes, export highlights.
6. **Journal:** Reading logs, thoughts, ratings, and quotes.
7. **Profile & Stats:** Reading challenges, streaks, genre distribution, and reading velocity.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance:** Low latency document rendering and smooth audio playback.
- **Security:** Secure authentication and end-to-end data privacy.
- **Offline Mode:** Local caching of active books and synchronization upon reconnecting.
